Task: "Forehead Scan" task — naya background-video (WebM) lagao + poora function detail mein
Context/background: DuckLock app mein ek task hai jisko "Forehead Scan" kehte hain — user ko is task ko complete karne ke liye ek fullscreen mock-video-jaisi screen dekhni padti hai ek fixed-time (abhi 30 seconds) tak, landscape-mode mein. Is screen ke background mein abhi koi random/placeholder-animation chal rahi hai jo humne pehle test-ke-taur-par lagayi thi.
Kya karna hai:
1. Naya video lagao: User ne khud ek naya video banaya hai (WebM format), jaan-boojh kar file-size chhota rakha hai — is naye video ko is task ke fullscreen-background mein lagana hai, purani placeholder-animation ki jagah.
2. Video quick-load/preloaded honi chahiye: Screen khulte hi video turant dikhni chahiye — koi visible loading-delay/fade-in nahi honi chahiye (jaisa poore app mein humne image/video-preloading ka rule rakha hai).
3. Video ko perfectly LOOP karna hai: Source-video sirf ~20 seconds ka hai, lekin task-duration abhi 30 seconds hai — matlab video ko poori task-duration tak seamlessly loop karna padega (ek se zyada baar chalegi). Zaroori: agar future mein kabhi task-duration badhayenge (jaise 30s se 2-minute), to ye looping-logic tab bhi bina kisi change ke properly kaam kare — video kitni bhi baar loop kare, koi jump/freeze/error nahi hona chahiye.
4. Existing Play/Pause button hi is video ko control karega: Is task ke liye pehle se ek play/pause toggle-button bana hua hai — usi button se is naye looping-video ka play/pause control hoga, koi naya/alag control mat banao.
5. ⚠️ Zaroori — Task complete hote hi video RUKNI chahiye: Jaise hi task complete/mark-as-done ho jaaye, ye looping-video automatically ruk jaani chahiye/unmount ho jaani chahiye. Aisa bilkul nahi hona chahiye ki task complete hone ke baad bhi video background mein permanently loop-mein-chalti rahe — isse phone ka resource (battery/CPU) waste hota rahega bina wajah ke. Task-completion ke saath video ka lifecycle bhi khatam hona chahiye.
Implementation-hint: expo-av/expo-video ke Video-component mein isLooping: true set karo (duration-agnostic, kisi bhi task-duration ke saath kaam karega). Task-complete hone par component ko unmount karo ya shouldPlay: false set karo — dono se video-loop stop ho jaayega turant.
